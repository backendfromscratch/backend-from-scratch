A free course to learn backend development from scratch: what happens behind every website and app, from the wires of the internet to AI agents. It's for anyone: you don't need to know backend or how to code to start, because every concept is explained from the beginning, with examples you can try yourself.

<CodeLens href="/en/phase-0/" label="phase-0/00-introduction.md" name="the Phase 0 introduction" />

## Even if backend isn't your goal

The first phases explain how the web works under the hood: how data travels across the internet, how you run a server from the terminal, and how the browser and the server talk over HTTP. That's useful to anyone who works with the web, not just people who write backend code.

- **If you do frontend,** you'll understand what happens after every `fetch` and why it sometimes fails.
- **If you design or work in product,** you'll know what your engineering team is talking about.
- **If you're curious,** you'll know what really happens when you open a website.

## Every lesson has something hands-on

- **Try it in your terminal:** guided exercises with real tools like `curl`, `dig` or `nc`.
- **Playgrounds:** real tools inside the site, like a database or cryptography, running in your browser.
- **Labs:** step-by-step simulations for the hardest things to see: HTTP, DNS, TCP and Docker.

The playgrounds and labs published so far, all together, in [playgrounds](/en/playgrounds/).
