Here are all the pieces of the course you can play with, with nothing to install. There are two kinds:

- **Labs:** step-by-step simulations of the hardest things to see, like the TCP handshake or a DNS lookup. You move at your own pace and can cause errors to see what happens.
- **Playgrounds:** real tools that run inside your browser, like the cryptography that protects HTTPS.

Each one lives in a lesson, in its "Try it" section, which explains what you are looking at. If something doesn't add up, start with the lesson.
