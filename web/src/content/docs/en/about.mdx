Hi, I'm Gianmarco. I've been doing frontend for almost ten years: screens, components and lots of `fetch` calls to servers I never really understood on the inside.

This course is how I'm learning it. I write each phase while I study it, and I explain it the way I wish someone had explained it to me: from the beginning, assuming nothing.

## How it's made

- **Phase by phase.** Phases are published when they're finished, in Spanish and English. What's coming is in the [syllabus](/en/roadmap/).
- **Tested before it's published.** The exercises are actually run, and the guided projects' code passes its tests on every change.
- **Free and open.** The lessons are licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/deed.en): you can share and adapt them as long as you credit the source. The code of the site and of the projects is on [GitHub](https://github.com/backendfromscratch/backend-from-scratch), under the MIT license.

## Contact

I'd love to hear from you: a question, some advice, something you miss or something that isn't clear.

- **Something about a specific lesson.** At the end of every lesson, after the questions, there's a link to tell me on GitHub. It arrives with the lesson already filled in.
- **Ideas, advice and questions.** In the [repository's Discussions](https://github.com/backendfromscratch/backend-from-scratch/discussions), a public forum: that way the answer helps more people.
- **No GitHub account, or something private.** Write to me at [hola@backenddesdecero.com](mailto:hola@backenddesdecero.com).
