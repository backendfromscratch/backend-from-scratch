# Cancellations and returns

## Cancelling an order

You can cancel one of your orders while it's **pending** or **paid**. If it was already paid, we refund the money to the same payment method. A **shipped** order can't be cancelled: in that case, wait until it arrives and return it.

## Returning a product

You have **30 days** from when you receive your order to return a product, as long as it's unused and still has its tag. Email support@shop.example with your order number and we'll tell you how to send it.

## When the refund arrives

Once we receive the return and check the product, we refund you within 5 to 10 business days, to the same method you paid with.

## Products that can't be returned

The gift card can't be returned, and neither can coffee beans once the bag is open. If a product arrives broken, email us a photo within 7 days of delivery.
