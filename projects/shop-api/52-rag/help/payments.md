# Payments

## How to pay

You pay on our payment gateway's page, by card. We never see your card number: we only receive the notice that the payment went through.

## When it's confirmed

As soon as the gateway tells us the payment succeeded, the order becomes **paid** and we send you a confirmation email. If the email takes a few minutes, don't worry: the order is already paid.

## If the payment fails

If the payment doesn't go through, the order stays **pending** and you aren't charged. You can try again from your account.

## Invoices

For now the shop doesn't issue invoices. If you need one, email support@shop.example with your order number.
