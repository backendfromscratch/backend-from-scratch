# Your account

## Creating an account

You need an email address and a password. We don't accept the most common passwords, because they're the first ones tried by anyone breaking into other people's accounts. At most 5 accounts per hour can be created from the same connection.

## Logging in

If you get your password wrong many times in a row, you'll have to wait a few minutes before trying again: that protects your account from anyone trying passwords one after another.

## What you can do with your account

See your orders and their status, change the quantity of a pending order, cancel a pending or paid order, and talk to the shop's assistant.

## Your data

We keep your email address and your orders so we can help you. We don't store your password as it is: we keep only a digest that can't be reversed.
