# Shipping

## What each order status means

- **Pending:** you've placed the order, but it isn't paid yet. While it's pending you can change the quantity or cancel it.
- **Paid:** the payment has arrived and we're packing your order. We send you a confirmation email as soon as the payment comes in.
- **Shipped:** the delivery company has the package. From here on it can't be cancelled.
- **Cancelled:** the order won't go ahead. The items go back into stock.

## How long it takes

We pack paid orders within one business day. The delivery company delivers them in 2 to 4 business days on the mainland. We don't have a published delivery time for other destinations.

## How much it costs

Shipping is included in each product's price: what you see when you order is what you pay.

## Where is my order

Log in and look at your orders: each one shows its current status. The assistant can tell you too if you give it the order number.
